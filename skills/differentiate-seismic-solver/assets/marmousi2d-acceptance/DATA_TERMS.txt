University of Houston AGL Marmousi-II data terms

The source archives are available from the University of Houston Allied
Geophysical Laboratory. Its data portal lists Marmousi II as Level 3 – Limited.
Consult the original terms for use and redistribution of the model and derived
material arrays. The skill provides preparation code and checksums. Source
archives and prepared NPZ files are generated or stored on the user's machine.

AGL data policy and dataset entry: https://www.agl.uh.edu/resources-data.php
Original model files: https://www.agl.uh.edu/downloads/downloads.htm
