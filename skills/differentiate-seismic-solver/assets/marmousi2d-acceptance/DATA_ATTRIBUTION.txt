The preparation script retrieves the three Model Definition SEG-Y archives
for Vp, Vs and density. It samples a 68 by 200 grid at 45 m spacing, converts
velocities and density to SI units and constructs the initial Vp with a 180 m
Gaussian (reflected edges) and the positive bulk-modulus floor for fixed Vs.
The last nominal depth of 3515 m samples the deepest source row at 3500 m.
The generated arrays match the inputs used in the paper's full-shot inversion.
